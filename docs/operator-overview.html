Transfer this booking to another operator

Use this when you have an internal agreement with another operator to cover this slot. Any operator with an approved vehicle on the same gate as this route can receive it. PITX staff still need to approve the handoff before it takes effect.

Change this request

Changing the route, date or time sends this booking back to PITX staff for approval and releases the assigned bay in the meantime — another operator may be approved into it. Setting the plate number alone does not affect its approval.

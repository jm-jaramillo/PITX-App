Devices

Test reads

Reads sent with "Test a vehicle" on Gate monitor are marked as tests. Clearing removes them and any trip arrival they recorded. Real gate reads are kept.

Bays in service

Trip Scheduling only assigns trips to bays in service, and the number of bays in service is the most trips it approves per 15-minute slot. Trips already on a bay keep it when the bay is taken out of service; move them in Trip Scheduling if needed. Bays are added and renamed in Trip Scheduling.

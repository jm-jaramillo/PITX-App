Approvals

Everything waiting on a staff decision - trip requests, vehicle registrations, and operator-to-operator transfers. Switch between them above; a red pin shows how many of each are waiting. Approving a trip request assigns the next free bay in the route's gate (change the dropdown first to pick another); Reject, with an optional note, is under the "..." menu. A transfer can only be approved once the receiving operator has confirmed.
